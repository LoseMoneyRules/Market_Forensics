# Market Forensics — Locked Product Baseline

Current pre-release line starts at **v0.0.1**. The product name is simply **Market Forensics**.

## Access names
- **FRIEND** — invited basic published access.
- **INSIDER** — invited deeper/full published research access.
- **CONTROL** — owner's private admin and research workspace.

V0.x is invite-only and free. Optional “Buy me a beer” support is separate from access and unlocks nothing.

## Separation rule
CONTROL is the complete private investing/research environment. FRIEND and INSIDER receive only deliberately published, non-personalized research.

`CONTROL -> PRIVATE RESEARCH -> VERSIONED SNAPSHOT -> PUBLICATION PREVIEW -> PUBLISH -> FRIEND / INSIDER`

Legal/publication constraints for external users must never remove useful private CONTROL functionality.

## CONTROL keeps
Portfolio, positions, cost basis, personal position sizing, risk budget, thesis, numerical invalidations, decision journal, private notes, watchlists, research engine, daily scans, deep research, research queue, valuation, Bear/Base/Bull, expectations, catalysts, flows, monitoring, source/data health, private exports, publication controls, user/access management and audit history.

## FRIEND sees
Published companies, score/status, executive thesis, selected KPIs, summary business/valuation/risk, key catalysts, limited history/monitoring, source/freshness context and a concise printable snapshot when enabled.

FRIEND cannot start research, manage tickers, access private CONTROL data or receive personalized portfolio sizing.

## INSIDER sees
Everything approved for FRIEND plus deeper published business/numbers, expectations, full valuation cases, bear case, catalysts, flows where redistribution rights permit, risk, monitoring, research history, evidence/source ledger, published snapshots and full published PDF research when implemented.

INSIDER cannot run the research engine, manage tickers or access private CONTROL data.

## Publishing
Published versions are immutable. New research creates a new version. Publication state is independent from private research state. Visibility must support PRIVATE / FRIEND / INSIDER. Disclosures can show that the author holds a position without exposing private size/cost unless deliberately published.

## Research posture
FRIEND/INSIDER are a research publication layer, not a personalized advisory engine. User wealth, personal portfolio, personal risk tolerance or circumstances are not used to generate individualized trade sizing or instructions.
